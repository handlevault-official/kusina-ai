## 🔥 About the Project

**Viral Food Caption & Promo AI** is a lightweight, fast, and mobile-friendly web application built specifically for **small food business owners**—including home bakers, milk tea and coffee shops, cloud kitchens, carinderias, and online food sellers.

Instead of spending hours thinking about what to post on social media, sellers simply enter their food item, optional promo details, target platform, and desired vibe—and the AI generates **3 high-converting Taglish captions** complete with emojis, strong Calls-To-Action (CTAs), and viral hashtags in seconds.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **📱 Multi-Platform Support:** Tailored copy for **Facebook & Instagram Posts**, **TikTok / Reels Short Hooks**, and **Messenger / Viber Suki Broadcasts**.
* **🤤 Custom Marketing Vibes:** Choose between *Cravings Mode (Nakakatakam)*, *Witty & Relatable*, or *Urgent / FOMO (Limited Slots)*.
* **🏷️ Promo & Offer Integration:** Automatically weaves prices, bundle deals (e.g., *Buy 1 Take 1*), and delivery promos into the copy.
* **📋 1-Click Copy:** Instant clipboard copy button for effortless posting on mobile or desktop.
* **⚡ Zero-Backend Architecture:** Pure client-side HTML/JS powered by the Google Gen AI SDK—fast, free to host on GitHub Pages, and easy to embed inside **Whop**.
